================================================================
  brief-presets.txt — TRADE _brief PRESETS
================================================================
  Drop one of these into the _brief.designDirection block of a
  fresh client-intake.json (skeleton), then tune per client.
  This is the layer that makes a painter feel different from a
  tiler. Content is trade-agnostic; DIRECTION is not.

  TWO WAYS TO USE THE KIT LINE:
    - LEAVE _brief.designKit EMPTY  -> the engine seeds a kit, so
      two painters look different. Best for building a varied
      portfolio of same-trade clients.
    - SET _brief.designKit = <primary>  -> locks the trade feel.
      Best for a one-off recon demo where you want it on-brand
      on the first build.

  PALETTE HEXES BELOW ARE STARTERS for a recon demo when you
  have no brand yet. Replace with the client's real colours the
  moment you have them. brandDark MUST stay genuinely dark: it
  paints the colour band AND satisfies the luminance guard
  (WCAG luminance <= 0.183). A light brandDark = no band.

  to seed unless the trade clearly wants one.

  PAINTER
================================================================
  designKit primary: coast      alt: press
  palette:  brand #1E4C8A   brandDark #0E2A50   accent #E07A2F   highlight #2FA36B
  vibe:  Clean, colour-confident, dust-free precision. Premium
         residential repaints and commercial work. Sells the
         FINISH, not the paint.
  voice: Precise and tidy. "Clean lines, furniture covered, no
         overspray, site left spotless." Confident about quality.
  audience: Homeowners doing a repaint/renovation; property
         managers; builders needing a reliable sub.
  motif: Single brushstroke arc, or a thin colour-swatch strip.
  avoid: Rainbow paint-splatter cliches. "3 rooms for $X"
         franchise pricing tiles. Roller-and-tin stock photos.

  TILER
================================================================
  designKit primary: signal     alt: ledger
  palette:  brand #3A3F45   brandDark #1B1E22   accent #C08A4E   highlight #4C9A8E
  vibe:  Geometric precision, waterproofing, pattern discipline.
         Bathrooms, kitchens, outdoor, large-format.
  voice: Level, square, sealed. "Laid dead level, waterproofed to
         Australian standard, grout lines you can trust."
  audience: Renovators; bathroom/kitchen clients; builders.
  motif: Tessellation / fine grid lines (subtle, behind nothing
         that carries text).
  avoid: Busy mosaic backgrounds. Tile-pattern wallpaper under
         copy. Anything that fights readability.

================================================================
  designKit primary: signal     alt: workshop
  palette:  brand #1466C7   brandDark #0A2B54   accent #F2B01E   highlight #2FA36B
  NOTE: if the client's brand is amber/yellow (common), keep

================================================================
  ADDING A NEW TRADE
================================================================
  1. Pick a primary kit by personality: workshop (heavy/solid),
     signal (technical/modern), coast (soft/approachable),
     press (refined/craft), ledger (editorial).
  2. Choose brand + a genuinely DARK brandDark (for the band).
  3. Write vibe / voice / audience / motif / avoid in the
     client's world, not generic marketing.
  4. Decide lock-vs-seed on designKit per the top of this file.
================================================================
